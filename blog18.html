Why Aesthetics & Wellness Belong Together

The connection between aesthetic treatments and measurable health outcomes is backed by a growing body of evidence. When patients invest in their appearance in a medically supervised setting, they are more engaged in their overall health — and we see it every day in our clinic.

A 2023 review published in the Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology found that patients who received aesthetic treatments reported significant improvements in psychological wellbeing, social confidence, and self rated health. These aren't superficial wins — they translate directly into reduced anxiety, improved sleep, and greater adherence to clinical care plans.

The Whole-Woman Difference

What sets Better with IJ apart from a standalone med spa is our integrative approach. Every aesthetic consultation begins with a conversation about your health — your hormones, your stress levels, your sleep, your nutrition. Because skin health doesn't exist in isolation.

Collagen production is affected by estrogen. Puffiness is linked to cortisol. Breakouts can signal metabolic imbalance.

Our clinicians don't just treat the surface. We look deeper — and that's where real transformation begins.
